About L&T Ahana Malad East

L&T Ahana Malad East Project Overview

L&T Ahana Malad East is a premium residential development by L&T Realty, featuring thoughtfully designed 2, 3 and 4 BHK luxury residences in Mumbai. The project brings together contemporary homes, lifestyle amenities and everyday conveniences at a well-connected address. Designed around the idea of “Connect More With Life,” L&T Ahana offers multiple levels of recreational and lifestyle spaces, including an E-Deck, clubhouse facilities and a Sky Arena. Integrated retail within the development further enhances the convenience of everyday living.

Located near the Western Express Highway, L&T Malad East offers convenient access to road, rail and metro networks, including Kurar Metro Station. Schools, hospitals, shopping destinations and major business hubs are also located within the surrounding area.
